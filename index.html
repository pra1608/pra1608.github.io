Prakhar holds a postgraduate degree in Manufacturing Engineering from IIT Bombay, specializing in additive manufacturing — his thesis modeled the physics behind laser Directed Energy Deposition, a process used to restore high-value molds, dies, and aerospace components.

He now works as a Deals professional at PwC, running end-to-end valuations — DCF, comparable company and transaction analysis — for clients spanning airports, satellite services, clean-tech, automotive, battery manufacturing, and sports franchises.

His work includes portfolio valuations for private equity and venture capital funds, mentoring analyst teams on market-based valuation methodology, supporting PwC's internal Strategy team, and reviewing valuations for audit clients — pairing an engineer's precision with a dealmaker's judgment.
