Research Spotlight

Numerical modeling of laser directed energy deposition

M.Tech thesis · Advisor: Prof. Ramesh Singh · IIT Bombay

Laser DED builds or repairs metal parts layer by layer: a laser melts a pool on the surface while powder is blown into it. Each pass re-heats the layers beneath, and that thermal cycling locks in residual stress that can crack a repaired mold, die, or aerospace part.

  • Melt-pool dynamics in Ansys Fluent to capture pool shape and dilution into the substrate.
  • Coupled metallurgical–thermomechanical 3D FE model in ABAQUS with DFLUX, USDFLD, UEXPAN and UMAT subroutines.
  • Outcome: a process window that delivers compressive residual stress with sound metallurgical bonding.

Valuation Methods

Discounted cash flow — intrinsic value

Value is the present value of the cash the business will generate. Forecast free cash flow to the firm, discount it at the weighted average cost of capital, and add a terminal value for the years beyond the forecast.

EV = Σ FCFFt / (1+WACC)t + TV / (1+WACC)n
TV = FCFFn+1 / (WACC − g)

Best for

  • Forecastable cash flows
  • Strategic / control decisions
  • FEMA and regulatory pricing

Watch out for

  • Terminal value often 60–80% of EV
  • High sensitivity to WACC and g
  • Optimistic management plans

How I build one

  1. Normalise historicals and build revenue, margin and capex drivers.
  2. Derive FCFF: EBITDA − tax on EBIT − capex − Δ working capital.
  3. Estimate WACC: CAPM cost of equity (beta from peers, country risk), post-tax cost of debt, target leverage.
  4. Terminal value by Gordon growth, cross-checked with an exit multiple.
  5. Bridge EV to equity value: less net debt and debt-like items.

Trading comparables — what the market pays today

Value the target off the multiples at which similar listed companies trade. Pick a peer set with similar business, growth, and margins, then apply the median or interquartile range to the target's metric.

EVtarget = Peer EV/EBITDA × EBITDAtarget

Best for

  • Market-anchored cross-check
  • Portfolio / fair-value reporting
  • Sectors with many listed peers

Watch out for

  • Peer selection drives the answer
  • Size, growth, calendarisation gaps
  • Liquidity discount for private targets

Precedent transactions — what acquirers have paid

Value the target off multiples paid in comparable M&A deals. Because those prices bought control, the result already includes a control premium, which makes it the natural benchmark for acquisitions.

EVtarget = Deal EV/EBITDA × EBITDAtarget

Best for

  • Control acquisitions
  • Setting a negotiation range
  • Fairness opinions

Watch out for

  • Deals from other market cycles
  • Limited disclosure of terms
  • Deal-specific synergies in price
